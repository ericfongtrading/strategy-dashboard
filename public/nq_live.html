Read the differences correctly

TheoreticalForward simulated outcomes for the selected rules. Estimated costs are assumptions, not broker charges. Historical backtest trades are not counted as live observations.
IB · futuresActual MNQ fills and reported commissions. NQ generates signals; MNQ is the traded contract. Separate sleeve positions are retained even when longs and shorts offset.
FTMO · CFDActual US100.cash ticket fills, commissions and swap. Lot size uses $100 / $200 nominal stop risk. Gaps and execution can change realized risk.
Slippage, costs and strategy details
Slippage ≠ instrument basisAdverse entry slippage = side × (fill − executable quote). Exit uses the opposite sign. Positive is worse. Each reference must be a fresh quote for that same instrument. NQ midpoint − venue midpoint is shown separately as basis.
No double-countingGross P&L already reflects execution prices, spread and slippage. Net = gross − commissions/fees + signed swap. We do not subtract slippage again. Missing costs show “—”; they are never assumed to be zero.
Selected refinementCCI-AM stop padding: 0.5 ATR. R3 waits two completed five-minute bars after a confirmed VL stop. CCI-AM has a stop and time exit, with no profit target. There is no $2,000 portfolio rule.

Dollar profits are affected by allocation. Compare net R only where measured initial risk exists. Unmatched or skipped signals remain visible rather than being silently discarded.
